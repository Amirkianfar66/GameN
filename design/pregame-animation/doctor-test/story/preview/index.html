The Doctor · story panels before the animation

The Last Two Doses

The medic of Colony Nine, told darkly, in eight stills. Mira plays the Doctor, drawn in the new hand-inked finish; everyone else is a shape with no face. The pictures carry no words. The lines under them are the story as I imagine it, for you to react to before any of it moves.

Each place, its own light

  • Colony Nine outside: rust dust, a pale sun, amber quarantine beacons, one lit door.
  • The ward: ochre walls under one swinging lamp, with a monitor's green trace.
  • The hatch: cold steel, lit by the departing ship's cyan-white searchlight, with the ward's amber behind her.
  • The case: one desk lamp and the two vials glowing.
  • Two beds: near black, with one cold doorway behind her and her shadow thrown toward us.
  • The seal: blue-grey steel under turning amber beacons, and a handprint on the glass from inside.
  • The climb: the cosmic language in saturated teal, magenta and gold, orbital rings and a far ringed giant, with the colony a ring of fire below.
  • Aboard: the film's own frame, the injector by her face.

What is drawn new

  • Seven new places in the new ink. Panel 8 is taken from the animation test.
  • Mira in seven new poses on the approved rig: walking, reading, a hand on the glass, standing between the beds, a palm on the door, and seated. Each pose has its own face and light.
  • Her case, her hand coming into the lamplight, and the people in the beds as faceless shapes.

Kept from the film's rules

  • No team colour in any picture; the cosmic colours stay clear of the three teams' colours.
  • The card's mark is the medical cross. Nobody but the player's own character has a face.
  • Panel 5 never shows what she chose.

# Frontend handoff: the board as the game (board motion, issue #87)

**Board targeting is owner-approved; the room scenes, the motion treatments (the hit among them) and full-body production adoption remain proposals (DSN-D30/D31/D32/D34/D35).** Issue [#87](https://github.com/Amirkianfar66/GameN/issues/87). Designed on base `94a49ce0c5220b814ec56333b028fbe6180b257e`, branch `codex/v1-pass-board-targets` ([PR #86](https://github.com/Amirkianfar66/GameN/pull/86), unmerged): protocol 2, engine `full-game-1.1.0`, ruleset `in-person-v1-pass-2026-10-08` (hash `a25cec29…7870183`), Original Powers off; legacy matches (`full-game-1.0.1`, `in-person-v1-2026-10-06`) have no Pass. Tokens 0.4.0, asset manifest `design-0.2.0`, unchanged.

The prototype is `design/board-motion/` (open it with `npm run dev:review --workspace @mothership/design-tokens`, then `http://127.0.0.1:4320/board-motion/`). Every action, cue, state, fact and component is listed in [board-motion-coverage.md](board-motion-coverage.md), generated from `design/board-motion/contract/`. What was run to verify it is in [board-motion-verification.md](board-motion-verification.md). The owner's request is recorded in [owner-decisions.md](owner-decisions.md#8-october-2026-the-board-as-the-game).

**Since 9 October 2026 the characters are full-body comic figures, and each room is a scene with five fixed places.** The owner asked for characters that feel more like the reference's comic page and said "lets try full body comic"; then asked for each room to be a scene, "like AAA game or animation art work", with five fixed places of five unique gestures (the Command Room one), and two animations for every gesture: moving to another room, and being hit ([owner-decisions.md](owner-decisions.md#9-october-2026-five-places-a-room-each-a-scene)). Every piece on the board is a figure drawn live by one rig in the gesture of the place it holds (21 places: C1, A1 to A5, B1 to B5, H1 to H5, J1 to J5); a move is getting up, a walk and settling into the new place; a hit is a burst and a jolt, then a flinch (Injured) or the place's fall (Eliminated). Everything else in this handoff (press areas, cues, words, secrets) is the same design, measured again. The scenes and the figures are a proposal asset set (section 9, GAP-5, GAP-7).

## 1. What changes for a player

The comic board stays on screen through a whole turn.

1. **Actions** in navigation opens a compact tray over the lower edge of the board, listing only the actions the player's own view opens. Move is not in it.
2. Choosing an action closes the tray. The characters the view offers for it get a dashed ring around their press area and a **+** tab; everyone else is drawn faint. The strip above navigation asks the release's question ("Choose a target", "Who is the Protection for?").
3. The player taps a **character, in its room** (Hospital and Jail included where offered). Several parts (Scan's guess, Supply's two, the Code's four) are counted in the strip; the picks of a set (Supply's two, the Code's four) are checked alike on the characters, never numbered, and named in seat order, the Code's four clipped into a Code card and Supply's two into shipping tags ([abilities-handoff.md](abilities-handoff.md)); Take back removes the last. Inline answers (Blue, Red, Alien; Abstain; No release request; Yes, No, Abstain) are buttons in the strip.
4. The strip asks to confirm, in the release's words; Confirm sends; the same neutral **Registered** stamp answers every action. Nothing about the outcome is drawn.
5. **Moving** is pressing a room's name tag: a dashed "You?" marks the place tentatively, the strip confirms, and the character moves only when the public update says so, on every screen at once. A room holding five (the owner's hard rule of 11 October 2026) says **FULL** on its tag on every screen and is never offered.
6. **Pass** is the middle of navigation, available only on the player's own ordinary turn, absent in legacy matches.

What every other phone and the shared display see during all of it: nothing, until a public fact changes (a move, a health, a phase).

## 4. The controller: what stays exactly as the release has it

The prototype mirrors `packages/presentation/src/model/connected-player.ts` and `apps/game/src/connected/action-flow.ts`; the board is only a new place to make the same choices.

- **What can be chosen** is only what the viewer's own view offers: `legalTargets[COMMAND]` for seats, `self.movementDestinations`, `self.releaseVoteAvailable`, `legalTargets.PASS_TURN`, and every seat for a Code attempt when `self.codeAttemptAvailable`. A character not offered has no press area. The engine never offers a player in the Command Room as a local target, nor the actor's own seat for a Shot, Disable or Hack; the fixtures follow it and the check holds them to it.
- **One command at a time.** While a command is in flight or registered-and-pending, Actions opens nothing new, a room tag says "Finish or cancel the current action first.", Pass is unavailable.
- **Steps**: choosing → confirming → submitting (or checking) → accepted, not accepted, or unknown, with the release's words for each. "Accepted" is only what the receipt says.
- **A choice that was not sent does not outlive its conditions** (`action-flow.ts`, the `choosing`/`confirming` branch of the view update). A closed panel, a stale view, time running out or a new phase drops it ("Your choice was not sent.") and every private mark with it. A fresh view that no longer offers what was picked asks to choose again from the start; an action the view no longer opens closes. The prototype draws the first (`robust.dropped`, and the flows background a page mid-choice) and the notice of the second (`robust.withdrawn`).
- **Sent and uncertain presentation is concealed too.** Background, a stale view, local expiry or page disposal removes every private board mark, Move ghost, strip, card, offer and private announcement. Unsent choices drop; submitted/checking/unknown/accepted recovery state stays internal. Foreground or a fresh view does not reopen it. An explicit Actions/Card press restores the same facts quietly; it sends/checks nothing and replays no receipt/card cue. The local stand-in counters measure that property, not durable Firebase behavior.
- **The connection banner** (ability art, slice 7). While the view may be out of date, `connection-banner` says so in the release's words over the top of the board, with Reconnect now, and the change is said once in the public live region; nothing plays. Every sheet keeps clear of it: the banner sets `--bm-banner-clear` to the height it covers, measured again whenever that height changes (its words grow with the reader's text, as the release's do), the sheets' `--bm-sheet-room` subtracts it, and the card, the tray and the result sheet cap their height to it, so a sheet's heading and Close stay in view at 320 x 568 (the board capture measures it). Actions is paused, as in the release. Reconnecting draws the same facts: nothing replays, and the fresh view's round and phase are said once. The card closes into the folder (the release's default); **where Integration keeps it open** ([DSN-REQ-16](integration-requests.md#dsn-req-16), recommended), it stays at rest, marked "May be out of date", its pictures not pressable and nothing in it moving, and an unsent choice still drops.
- **Synthetic offer gates.** Code is Hacker-only in Round 5; Supply is Supplier-only in Round 3; only Captain may move to Command, before voting and while Healthy/free. Election starts after Round 1 with Healthy/free candidates; all living players, including Injured/Jailed, vote. Role/phase/actor gates and the injured self-Rescue exception are checked. Resources, earlier commands and full match reachability are not established. Crowds are labeled layout stress.
- **Existing acknowledgment sidecar.** Consume the authorized own acknowledgment and seat-session pairing for Supplier results and recipient-only receipts, including `historyAvailable=false`; synthetic knowledge prose is not that typed feed. Hack requester is the public active seat; only the partner association is private. No new fact, audience or public cue is requested.
- **A pending Hack keeps its full minute**: the Hack phase shows its own 60 s timer.
- **Move** stays `openRoomMovement()`: the tag opens the move action, chooses the destination if offered, and says "You cannot move to that room right now." if not. The piece is drawn where the view says, never where the player asked.
- **Pass**: confirm in the strip, a neutral receipt ("Turn passed."), the next phase in the status bar.

## 5. Layout: places, scenes and bands

All of it is computed on every device from public facts only, so every screen draws the same. The functions are `design/board-motion/js/layout.js` (who is where, pure and Node-testable), `design/board-motion/scenes/frame.mjs` (how a scene is framed in a panel), `geometry()` and `pressRect()` in `design/board-motion/js/board.js`, and `fitBoard()` in `design/board-motion/js/app.js`; the places are in the scenes, `design/board-motion/scenes/<room>.mjs`.

**Places** (`layout.placements()`, `assign()`, `gestureOf()`). Each room has five fixed places, the Command Room one: a point of the scene (`at`, in its 1024 x 768 units), a scale, a drawing depth, a gesture, a mirror and the place's fall. The Command Room's place is the Captain's. Elsewhere the places are a function of who is in the room now, and of nothing else: the lowest seat first, each takes the first free place in its own order of preference, seat `n` starting at place `(n - 1) mod 5`. So a screen that has just loaded or reconnected draws exactly what a screen that followed the match draws; keep no history of places. Seats `n` and `n + 5` prefer the same place and the lower seat gets it. So when someone comes in or leaves, a character can step to another place in its room: the lower seat of its pair took its place, or a place it prefers came free (`layout-reflow`, the move's walk without its trail and puff).

**Gestures.** The place's own, mirrored where the place says. Injured: the same gesture marked `+injured`, which draws a bandage round the head (and H5's sling); a healed character in the Hospital wears none. Eliminated: the place's fall: slumped over the desk (A1) or the bench (B4, B5), still where it sits or lies (`+still`: H1, H2, H3, H5, J1, J4, J5), or lying on the deck. Nothing else chooses a gesture.

**Five a room is a hard rule** (the owner, 11 October 2026; GAP-2, DSN-D27). The owner also decided its six cases (a full room not offered and a move into it refused; the Eliminated count; a sixth Injured player waits where it is for a free Hospital place; the Jail never exceeds five; starting rooms random, at most five a room; a return to a full room goes to the other of Room A and Room B). The engine does not enforce it yet; Backend is asked to ([DSN-REQ-12](integration-requests.md#dsn-req-12), issue [#96](https://github.com/Amirkianfar66/GameN/issues/96)). The board shows it now: a room holding five, the Eliminated among them, says FULL on its tag (`CAPACITY` in `layout.js`, from public occupancy only): Room A and Room B, and the Hospital and the Jail, so the table sees why an Injured character waits where it is. The fixtures never offer a full room (the check refuses one). Should a view still carry six to nine in one room, the board draws it rather than break: they stand in a 3 x 3 crowd, in one of three stances by seat number (`stand`, `standFolded`, `standBack`), mirrored right of the middle (layout stress). The Final Zone, which is not a room, stands everyone in two rows the same way.

**Framing a scene** (`frameScene()`). The scene's focus (the bay its places are in) spans the panel's width, and the picture always covers the panel: `k = max(width / focus width, width / inner width, height / inner height)`. Vertically the panel shows the band from the back row's heads to the front row's press areas under the room's caption, or, on a panel too short for all of it, keeps the press areas and lets the back row's heads go under the caption. What is drawn in front of a place is a layer of its own (`.bm-layer[data-layer]`), stacked by depth between the figures: a figure at depth `d` is drawn at `2d`, a layer at `2d + 1`, a figure's front layer at `1500 + d`. These are z-index values and must be integers.

**Press areas** (`pressRect()`). Every place's press area is a square of `max(44px, min(64px, 0.26 x standing height x unit))` on its gesture's press point (the upper chest; the head in a bed or on the cot), moved inward to stay whole in the panel and below the caption. It is the place's: an Eliminated character keeps the press area of the place's gesture whatever its fall, so the layout never changes with a health and a Code attempt can still reach it. The check (`design/tools/board-motion-check.mjs`) lays out every set of taken places, alive and Eliminated, at the 141 px of a half-width panel on a 320 px phone (306 px for the Command Room), and refuses any that would need more than its band there for whole, separate press areas.

**Band heights** (`fitBoard()`). For each room: its caption (a move tag's button, at least 44 px; a fixed caption's height plus 6 px; plus 16 px while a public label is shown over a head), then the smallest height, in 2 px steps from the caption plus 50 px, at which every taken place's press area is whole, inside and apart; an empty room keeps a sliver. A band takes the larger of its two rooms; what is left is shared 0.8 : 1.25 : 1 (top, middle, lower), a band growing no taller than its scenes can fill without cropping their places off the sides, past which the middle band takes the rest. Measure the caption, do not assume it: at enlarged text it grows.

**Short phones** (app height 640 px or less; in the runtime, where the app fills the viewport, `@media (max-height: 640px)`): status 44 px, navigation 56 px, gutter 4 px, the strip's words 13/11 px. Measured at 320 x 568 with the strip in every state (section 10).

**Enlarged text** (width under 18 em): the board scrolls inside itself and is never clipped; names leave the name plates, numbers stay; the strip's answers may wrap.

## 6. The strip

`section.bm-strip` sits in its own grid row above navigation, so the board shrinks and nothing is covered.

- The action's name (`.bm-strip__kind`, a slanted caption) is set into the question; the question is `#bm-strip-line`, focused after every step (`tabindex="-1"`, `aria-live="polite"` on the strip).
- **One control at the side** (`.bm-strip__side`): Cancel or "Take back Player N" while choosing; Done, OK or Check again after the answer.
- **One row beneath** (`.bm-strip__controls`): inline answers of equal width; a single answer (Abstain, No release request) shares the row with Cancel; a long "Take back Player N" with no answer takes the row itself; Confirm (primary) with Choose again.
- Progress of a Supply: two shipping tags at the start of "Chosen so far: …", the picks' chips in seat order (ability art, slice 3). Of a Code attempt: the Code card, its windows filled in seat order (ability art, slice 2).
- The receipt's stamp sits on the strip's top edge, clear of the words: where it reaches over their column, the first line wraps short of its corner (38, 60 and 80 px for Registered, Not accepted and Sending in the prototype, measured; the board capture checks no stamp covers a word). The release's receipt words are the line and its detail.

## 7. Motion

Every cue, with trigger, audience, facts, anchor, duration and easing, cancellation, settled state, reduced-motion form and asset, is in [board-motion-coverage.md](board-motion-coverage.md#cues) and `design/board-motion/contract/cues.json`. Six are the reviewed cues of `design/contract/motion-cues.json`, unchanged in duration and level: `cue-selection`, `cue-registration`, `cue-public-move`, `cue-status-change`, `cue-phase-change`, `cue-round-transition`. The others are proposals: `cue-tray-open`, `cue-strip-enter`, `cue-target-eligible`, `cue-target-pick`, `cue-pending`, `cue-not-accepted`, `cue-unknown`, `cue-room-press`, `cue-move-tentative`, `cue-sheet-open`, `cue-turn-accent`, `cue-tally`, `cue-ballot-subject`, `cue-public-hit` (new with the owner's decision, GAP-9), `cue-end-reveal` (the match's end, ability art slice 6) and `layout-reflow` (now a state, not a motion). Every duration is a token of 0.4.0 (`selection` 120, `registrationStamp` 120, `cardTransition` 220, `roundTransition` 700, `pieceMove` 900 with its beats 120 / 450 / 330, `publicImpact` 320, `comicBeatMaximum` 900 with its beats 240 / 260 / 400, `reducedMotionFade` 80); no token changes.

**The two animations of every gesture** are generated from it by the rig (`design/board-motion/figures/motion.mjs`: `blend()` turns every bone from one skeleton to another, `walkPose()` generates the walk with two-bone legs, `jolt()`, `flinch()` and `falling()` work from whatever gesture the character holds, and `TIMELINES` sequences them), so a new place's gesture has both at once. Each frame is drawn from one clock, a Web Animation on a node of the choreography, so that pausing every animation at a time (the storyboards, `?play=…&t=…`) stops the drawing at that time.

**The public move** extends `createComicMotion()` and keeps every rule it has: positions taken before the redraw; `movedPublicSeats()` (four or fewer moves in one update, else none); no motion when the page is not visible, after a gap of more than a second, on the first or a reconnected snapshot, or with reduced motion. What changes, in the beats of `motionBeatsMs.pieceMove`: the character gets up from its place (120 ms, drawn on an echo of it in the old room, between that room's layers), walks across in a layer over the board (`.bm-fx`, `pointer-events: none`, 450 ms, facing the way it goes, from its feet where it was to its feet at its new place, eased in and out), and settles into the new place's gesture on its own piece (330 ms). An Injured character limps, bandaged. The piece's plate and markers wait with it (opacity 0, still read out). An ink trail and a landing puff (approved `fx-ink-trail.paper`, `fx-landing-puff.paper`) fade by 900 ms. Nobody else in either room moves: everyone keeps their place.

**The public hit** (`cue-public-hit`, a proposal, GAP-9) belongs to a public health change to Injured or Eliminated, nothing else: a burst (`.bm-hit`, a star in the tokens' ink and paper at the character's chest, `motionMs.publicImpact`) peaks with a jolt of the gesture at 80 ms. Injured where it is, it flinches and settles back into its gesture, now with a bandage, by 720 ms; if the same update moves it (the Hospital at the end of the round), it walks out hurt and limps there. Eliminated, it falls into the place's fall in the beats of `motionBeatsMs.comicBeatMaximum` (240 / 260 / 400 ms). Every hit in one update starts at the same moment, so the engine's order is not shown. No lettering, weapon, shooter, trajectory or direction.

**The match's end** (`cue-end-reveal`, a proposal, ability art slice 6) belongs to the view's phase turning `FINISHED` or `ABORTED` on a page that drew the match before it ended: the result sheet rises (240 ms), the winner is struck on its ribbon (260 ms) and, at a finished match, the cast list's rows turn up one after another, each Code pin stamped on its row, the Code's sentence last (400 ms): `motionMs.comicBeatMaximum`, 900 ms in all. An ended match has the rise alone. It marks only what the sheet already says in words, and every word is in the document (and said once by the public live region) from the first frame. A page that loads at the end, a duplicate snapshot, a reconnect or the menu closing plays nothing; reduced motion (the device's or the app's, even switched on mid-reveal) draws the sheet at once, with the 80 ms fade.

**Rules Frontend must keep** (the prototype's director, `design/board-motion/js/director.js`, is a working reference):

- Public cues start only from a difference between two drawn public views. Same revision: nothing. A newer change to a seat withdraws that seat's cue in flight; the latest view is what is drawn.
- No cue is queued, none blocks a control or a deadline, and none changes state: animation completion never submits, spends or advances anything.
- Reduced motion (the device setting or the in-app one): no walk, trail, jolt, fall, lift, shake or zoom. Arrival, a hit's new gesture, status changes, stamps and sheets use the 80 ms fade. Same facts, same controls.
- Nothing repeats; nothing moves while nothing happens; no camera motion.

## 8. Secrets: the checklist for the implementation

- Private cue rules (`[data-target]`, press areas, pick numbers, the tentative move) are scoped to the viewer's own board, so a private element that leaked into a public board would still not be drawn. The check enforces this in the prototype's stylesheet.
- The same **Registered** stamp and the release's receipt words for every action: no per-action effect, color, sound or vibration.
- No cue on any public surface for a shot, Disable, Protection, Rescue, Hack request, Scan, Supply or Code attempt; a health change gets one hit, the same for every cause and from no direction, with no attacker, weapon, projectile, BANG, block or giver. Protection stays the Undercover's private fact, including to its recipient.
- Public facts only from `PUBLIC_MOVE`, `PUBLIC_HEALTH_CHANGED`, `PHASE_CHANGED` and the public view (`seats[]`, `activeSeatId`, `round`, `phase`, `ballot.releaseTargetSeatId`, `lastTally`). The full fact-to-source table is in [board-motion-coverage.md](board-motion-coverage.md#facts-and-where-they-come-from).
- No sound, haptics or per-action asset request. The scenes are public art in one stylesheet loaded once with the public board; the figures are drawn live, fetching nothing; the role devices stay in the roles bundle and the private card.
- The card and its device exist in the document only while the card is open; the private live region is emptied when nothing private is open.
- **The end reveal** (`endReveal.roles`, `endReveal.code`) is drawn only in the result sheet, only at `FINISHED`, and only from the public view: never from a seat's own view, never on the board's pieces, never as device art. A row's side is `data-end-faction`, from the role the end reveal names; the check refuses an end hook outside `.bm-result`, and the capture refuses a role word or an end hook anywhere else or before the match finished. `ABORTED` carries neither, and the sheet says only the host's sentence. As in the release, Card and Actions are closed for good at the end, so nothing private is drawn beside it.
- A character's look never selects a rule: the stylesheet keys characters only for their picture.
- A figure's place and gesture come only from public facts: who is in the room, by seat number, and the places drawn before; the Captain; Injured; Eliminated. Nothing private chooses a place, a gesture, a stance or a facing; the check looks for private words in `layout.js`. Every gesture is drawn by the rig from the code every device loads, whatever the role, so drawing one never fetches anything.
- **Verify** on the real runtime what the prototype verified on itself (section 10): with one phone choosing, sending and receiving for every action, every other client's DOM holds no private mark and both boards agree where everyone stands.

## 10. Tests Frontend should add

1. For every action kind: the eligible characters are exactly `legalTargets[COMMAND]` (every seat for Code), each with its own press area, none in the Command Room or the actor's own seat where the engine excludes them.
2. Every press area at least 44 x 44, on screen, inside its room, hit at its centre by itself, overlapping no other and no room tag, at 320 x 568, 360 x 740, 390 x 844 and 430 x 932, with all nine in one room.

4. Two clients: for every action and step, the other client's DOM has no private hook, press area, pick number, tentative move, stamp or role word.
5. Replay of the same snapshot plays nothing; a reconnect plays nothing and stops what was in flight; five moves in one update fly none; reduced motion flies none and draws the same facts.

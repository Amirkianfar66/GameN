## Lifecycle rules (every scene)

1. Clock: t = server time now - the occurrence's publishedAt (an event) or the phase's startedAt (a stage). Never a local start time, never an animation's end. The server's endsAt and expiresAt are unchanged by any presentation.
2. Late load or reconnect: if t >= the film's end, draw the final state at once (no replay); if 0 <= t < end, seek to t; a stage presentation (capsule, vote result) snaps to its current pose. Never replay a historical death, Sync or cinematic.
3. Duplicates: key every presentation by its event id (or phase id); one id plays once per page. A repeated or older viewRevision draws nothing new. An event past expiresAt (publishedAt + 30 s) never starts.
4. Cancellation: a newer viewRevision that changes what a presentation shows (a seat moved, a phase ended) cancels it: remove its nodes and draw the latest authoritative state. Cancelling never changes game state.
5. Precedence: terminal result > Sync > Captain commands > medical outcome (death before rescue at one berth) > attack, Blackout and blocked > movement and placement > ambient idles. A higher presentation starting in the same region cancels a lower one to its final state; different regions (another room panel) play concurrently.
6. Hidden tab: pause nothing on the server; on return compute t again from the server clock and seek or snap. Audio never plays in a hidden tab.
7. Interaction: press areas, name tags, room labels, timers, Pass and navigation never move, scale or hide for a film; a film may darken or shake only the art layer. Pointer events pass through every film layer.
8. Gameplay visibility (the owner's review of 6 November 2026): by default every film is a board treatment, on phones and the display alike: drawn in the board's own layers (a room's art, the board's film band), under the press areas, slot hits, room labels, timers, Pass and bars, so it covers nothing a player selects or reads. A presentation that covers the board (a display cut, a comic page) plays only in a phase where nobody decides (HACK_RESULT, VOTE_RESULT, FINISHED, ABORTED; design/cinematic/lib/visibility.mjs) and inside that phase's own time; in any other phase it gives way to its board treatment from its first frame. No film waits for, extends or takes time from a phase. One film has a stage of its own (owner decision P27, 6 November 2026): after an actual Blackhole Sync, a separate six-second presentation stage, then the full 60-second Jail vote; only the Blackhole's stage cut plays there (its phase kind is R16; until a view carries it, the board treatment plays at the Jail vote's start). No other stage exists: in particular no covering EJECT stage is approved, so the EJECT's display cut is a review study that nothing mounts (design/cinematic/lib/visibility.mjs PRESENTATION_STAGES, NO_RUNTIME_COVER).
9. Reduced motion: honoured at load and when changed during playback (a change mid-film switches to the reduced variant at the same t). Reduced variants carry the same information with fades of 80-120 ms, no travel, camera, shake, flash, particles or spin.
10. Audio: public events only, from the cue sheet (never only inside a video); muted by the device setting; private, room-filtered and pair-private presentations play no public audio by default.
11. Asset failure: a missing picture or video never blocks: the scene's static final state (or its words in the live region) stands alone.

## 1. The dropout in the exported Rescue video

**Cause.** The renderer captured a frame as soon as the stage reported its seek done. When the
display cut's camera changed the scale of the large ward and room pictures (SVG `<image>`
elements), Chromium decoded and painted their tiles asynchronously. A capture could land before the
tiles did, so a few frames lacked the ward's back and beds. One such frame was in the camera's
push-in near 3.1 s.

**Fix.** The renderer now does two things:
- It starts Chromium with `--disable-checker-imaging`, so pictures are decoded before the frame that
  paints them.
- It captures every frame until two consecutive captures are byte-identical (at most 8 looks).

Each render record now carries `settling { rule, resettled, unsettled }`. A frame that never settles
is listed there and printed as a warning.

**Also affected.** A frame-by-frame scan of every committed video found the same dropout in the
runtime drone insert: the ceiling picture was missing at 1.00 s and 1.17 s. The insert is
re-rendered too; its frame function is unchanged.

**Evidence.** Every one of the 27 pilot videos was scanned frame by frame (2,905 frames) for
one- or two-frame dropouts, where a run differs from the frames on both sides while those two agree.
None was found.

The render records show what the settling did:
- Every re-rendered frame settled, except two in the Blackhole's stage cut (2,133 and 2,267 ms, in
  the title hold). Their captures kept differing by filter raster noise. Both frames were checked by
  eye: nothing is missing.
- 7 of the 8 Rescue storyboard stills from before the release, and all 15 Blackhole and capsule
  stills, re-rendered byte-identical. The pass is deterministic away from the changes.

## 2. Gameplay visibility

`design/cinematic/lib/visibility.mjs`, and a new line in the motion bible's lifecycle:

- **The default is the board treatment, on phones and the display alike.** It is drawn in the board's
  own layers: a room's `.v2-art`, the board's film band. It sits under the name tags (3500), markers,
  press areas (4000), slot hits (4200), room labels (5000), timers, Pass and bars (6400). It covers
  nothing a player selects or reads, so it may play in any phase.
- **A covering form only where nobody decides.** This covers a display cut and a comic page.
  `presentationIn(phase.kind)` returns `cover` only in `HACK_RESULT`, `VOTE_RESULT`, `FINISHED` and
  `ABORTED`, and the form must fit inside that phase's own time. Any other phase, or an unknown one,
  gets the board treatment from its first frame.
- **No time is taken.** Nothing waits for, extends or takes time from a phase. A presentation stage of
  its own would be a new phase and is not assumed.

Per pilot:

- **Rescue (Pilot 1).**
  - `RESCUED` is published as the navigation stage or the next round opens, with decisions open. So the
    display's default is now the board treatment on its own Hospital panel, as on a phone. The
    camera cut is the `cut` surface, for a stage where nobody decides.
  - The cinematic pass on the board is drawn under every name tag and press area. The approved S5
    layers (the inset, drone and pulse) keep their own z and their keep-clear placement, clear of
    the room label and the other occupants' berths.
  - The pass is masked by S5's keep-clear areas (the room label and the other occupants' berths). No
    light, ring or spark crosses a neighbour.
  - The board's grade tints only the room's art. THUMP and BEEP are lettered only in the cut.
- **Blackhole (Pilot 2).**
  - By default it plays behind the crew, on every surface, for 2.4 s from the Sync, inside the Jail
    vote's first seconds. Each room's back wall gives way to one shared field of space: the blackhole
    forms at the board's centre, the ship streaks behind the rooms into it, the flare follows and the
    walls return.
  - It is drawn at `z-index: 1` in each room's art layer, under every scene layer, figure, tag, label,
    press area, timer and bar. The walls never go fully dark (88 % at most).
  - No title is drawn behind the crew: the client's words name the event.
  - The vote's `endsAt` is unchanged, and the reduced still is 1.44 s.
  - The 5.2 s cut with the ink bloom, title and wipe is the `stage` variant (display only), for a
    presentation stage the owner may approve (R1, open). It is never the default.
- **Private Talk (Pilot 3).**
  - The exit is S2's approved exit on the board on every surface, the display included: 2.23 s plus a
    0.3 s settle, over whatever phase comes next, covering nothing. The display's exit page is
    removed (R2 answered).
  - The entrance page plays in `HACK_RESULT` (5 s, nobody decides) and is gone at 4.9 s.
  - The talk is S2's seated loop in the board's capsule panel.

The film follows the published snapshot's placement. The two review fixtures:
- `free`: Rigel, healed at H1, free, back in Room A at A2.
- `detained`: Mira, healed at H2, still detained, back in the Jail at J1.

- **Full, 6.2 s** (was 5.6 s; the brief suggests 5 to 6 s, and the walk adds 0.2 s past that).
  - S5 runs unchanged to the patient sitting up.
  - The patient stands beside the bed (4.8–5.12 s) and walks out of the ward by the nearer side

    the capsule's prisoners do.
  - The berth is empty from the moment the patient is off it: no screen, no lamp of its own.
  - On the board, the patient's playable figure at its snapshot place is drawn throughout. It is
    greyed as away while the film shows the patient at the berth, and settles back in colour as the
    walker leaves (5.8–6.05 s).
  - The cut ends on a closing panel: the destination room drawn as the board draws it, with the
    patient at their slot in crew clothes (Room A or B) or prison clothes (the Jail).
- **Short, 1.6 s:** the patient stands and leaves the berth at 1.08–1.46 s.
- **Reduced, 1.76 s:** S5's two stills, then a third (the empty berth) with 80 ms fades.
- Nothing leaves the patient apparently occupying a Hospital bed. Only if a snapshot still had the
  patient at that berth would the film end there (`leaves: false`).
- No rule changed: the server's placement is the only authority (request R3 answered).

## 4. Promise evaluation

Request R6 previously said every Promise result arrived "at the evaluation (voting start after
Sync)". That was wrong. Each Promise is evaluated at its own opportunity (P04, P23, P24):

| Promise | Evaluated |
| --- | --- |
| Meet in A, Meet in B | When its round's voting begins after Sync, on the synced places |
| Jail Vote, No Jail Vote | When its round's Jail vote closes, on the actual ballot (not the outcome or admission) |
| Captain Support | When the specified election closes: every ballot, runoffs included |
| Officer Selection | At that election and its Officer appointment |

Same-round Promises finalize in their round. Only election Promises may stay Pending until their
election or the match end. R6 now asks that each `promises[].status` change arrive with the
`viewRevision` that closes its own opportunity.

Group 4 (#178) already words each card's "Decided" line per opportunity. Its remaining "at the
evaluation after Sync" phrases are corrected when this change reaches it.

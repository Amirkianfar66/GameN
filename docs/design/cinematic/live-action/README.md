# Live-action insert studies

**Status of every study here: NOT PRODUCED.** No footage was filmed, generated or licensed for
GameN. No video tool, camera, stock library or paid service was used or authorized. What exists is
the pre-production package: a treatment, a shot list on the comic film's own beats, storyboard
frames rendered from the comic pilot (design art, not footage), exact production prompts, a grade
and composite specification, and a provenance and rights plan. A future producer can shoot or
generate from these; the comic pilots remain the delivered, working treatment.

| Study | Comic pilot it would replace or intercut | Insert slot | File |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Medical drone deploy | Pilot 1, Hospital rescue | the ceiling cut-in (600 to 1150 ms) and the identity-free drone insert | [hospital-drone.md](hospital-drone.md) |
| Blackhole passage | Pilot 2, Blackhole and Sync | the space shot (620 to 4220 ms) and the identity-free space inserts | [blackhole-passage.md](blackhole-passage.md) |
| Private Talk | Pilot 3, capsule | none: comic only by design (see why) | [private-talk.md](private-talk.md) |

Rules every insert keeps (the production brief, section 2): no player name, character face that
stands for a player, role, side, outcome, control or variable text; a poster and the comic
animated alternative for every video; never required to understand or complete a turn; graded and
composited into Comic Noir, not a photorealistic replacement of the board.

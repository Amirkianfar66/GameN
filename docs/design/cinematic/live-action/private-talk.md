# Live-action study: Private Talk

**NOT PRODUCED, and not proposed for production.** The capsule's presentation is comic only by
design, and this note records why, so the gap is a decision rather than an omission.

- The pair is public but their conversation is private: live-action people walking into a room and
  talking would invite reading lips, gestures or emotion into a scene whose content no audience may
  see. The comic seated loop is deliberately speaker-neutral (S2: no mouth, no speaker cue).
- The participants are the players' chosen crew characters; a live actor would stand for a player's
  identity in a way the comic characters do not, and would differ per match only by recasting.
- The entrance must fit inside the five-second result stage and snap to the current pose on a late
  load: frame functions (the comic page, S2) do this exactly; a fixed video cannot.

If the owner wants a practical flavour later, the one safe insert is an identity-free plate of the
capsule's door: the door sliding open and sealing, the busy light coming on (no people), graded
like the other studies. Prompt (for a shoot or an authorized generator): "A narrow steel cabin door
in a navy-violet spaceship corridor slides open with a pneumatic hiss and closes again; a small
amber light above it switches on; graphic-novel lighting, no people, no text, 3 seconds, 4K."

# Designer deliverables

**Version 9, 19 October 2026.** One index of what the Visual and Motion Designer has delivered for the owner's motion specification of 13 October 2026 and for the setup art, and of what is still to be delivered. Integration asked for it in its [renewed review of #144](https://github.com/Amirkianfar66/GameN/pull/144#issuecomment-6170610912) and again, complete and versioned, in its [completion request](https://github.com/Amirkianfar66/GameN/pull/144#issuecomment-6171018083). The versions are listed at the end.

- **Delivered** means a reviewable proposal: a pushed branch and its draft PR, with its doc, review pictures, checks and exact hashes.
- **Adoption is not the Designer's.** Integration approves files by name and hash, pins them and pairs art with rules. Frontend builds the renderer that draws them. Nothing here claims either.
- **The live release, as of 19 October 2026** (Integration's read of the release metadata on #161): source `6ca4b51`, engine `full-game-1.10.0`, art `design-0.4.0`. That art release adopts the phone kit (`5c9a151`), board v2 at #144's `7786b91` (the fixed slots, the roster-sized ward, the Blackout's lighting, ATTACK BLOCKED and the private capsule) and #116's setup art (`27be874`). It excludes the medical films, the Captain's films, the Clone and the four-berth Hospital, delivered after it. The successor candidate that draws them is separate and not activated: Backend's #163, the art and source registration #164, the drawing approval #165 and Frontend's playable client #166 (`c35b9eca`). (Earlier versions of this index recorded staging as source `37f573a`, engine 1.5, `abilities-0.7.0`, with neither #116 nor board v2.)

The storyboard numbers S1 to S15 and the production plan come from the Designer's review of 13 October 2026, published unchanged as [motion-review-2026-10-13.md](motion-review-2026-10-13.md). The owner approved the plan's order that day ([owner-decisions.md](owner-decisions.md#13-october-2026-the-fixed-slots-the-ward-and-the-smallest-phone)), then asked for S2 ahead of it on 14 October. Where the review and the specification packet differ, the packet controls ([motion-spec-crosswalk.md](motion-spec-crosswalk.md)). The packet itself is in [evidence/owner-motion-spec-2026-10-13/](evidence/owner-motion-spec-2026-10-13/PROVENANCE.md).

# Food return: release ordinary navigation from historical request closures

14 November addendum: [Designer accepted exact native 8ff with no blocker](https://github.com/Amirkianfar66/GameN/pull/240#issuecomment-6876930457). Their full verdict identifies the narrow Pass box/Close wrapping below as a development mount artifact, now corrected in the [live-mount supplement](food-supply-live-mount-2026-11-14.md). Bottom-menu wrapping remains the existing #222 follow-up. The [final connected matrix](food-supply-final-matrix-2026-11-14.md) still fails acceptance: seven cancelled for disconnect; eight/nine passed. The original pinned a09 packet and its evidence files remain unchanged.

Clean native candidate **`8ffce95a0cfe92facc75d8cf0a86f3686608f176`** corrects the persistent Food overlay found after all three [841 first connected cases](food-supply-native-first-841-2026-11-14.md). A retained request closure from the completed Food step could continue owning Food chrome after the current card and Task had gone. Ordinary navigation stayed hidden and inert even when the authoritative ordinary model offered Pass. The correction requires that closure to belong to the current round and current Food step, matching the existing current-card guard.

The [previous native 952 visual approval](https://github.com/Amirkianfar66/GameN/pull/240#issuecomment-6869904727) covers the earlier candidate. **This 8ff runtime correction needs renewed focused Designer approval in #240.** The earlier failed connected packets retain their original outcomes; no new 8ff connected case or acceptance matrix has run.

## Exact change and inputs

Only `apps/game/hosted/planet/food-render.mjs` changes among the 30 native inputs relative to approved `952e7d9ca5766aa3e9514a362c86a3f91b20e0fa`: SHA-256 **`0a55d7499b2942ff96e65ce7f8b304c2b9b0fea37e982b8081e73993aba52cc6`**, 35,737 bytes, gzip 11,812. The other 29 inputs, 14 pinned Designer sources and 37 SVG candidates are unchanged. The controller, original receipts, history, game rules, timing, artwork, CSS, layout and words are unchanged.

The guard adds `c.round === view.round` and `c.foodStepId === view.step?.stepId` to the historical-closure branch of chrome ownership. It neither deletes historical closures nor infers a new result for an old request. An invitation/current closure/current participant Task still owns Food chrome; a closure from a completed step cannot hide the ordinary controls.

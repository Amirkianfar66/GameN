# Participant questionnaire

Filled in by each player, alone and in silence, straight after the match and before anyone talks about it. It takes about three minutes. Answers go into `experience` in [the match record](match-record.template.json), under the player's pseudonym.

Give players this text:

> This is about the game, not about how well you played. There are no right answers. Please do not write your name, and please do not write anything that was said in a private Hack.

**Your pseudonym** (from the facilitator): P-__________

**Before today, how many times had you played Mothership?** ______

**How often do you play hidden-role or bluffing games?** never / sometimes / often

Circle one number. 1 means "not at all" and 5 means "completely".

| | | | | | | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. I understood the rules well enough to play. `clarity` | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | |
| 2. I understood what my own role could do. `understoodOwnRole` | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | |
| 3. On my turns I knew what my options were. `knewWhatToDoOnTurn` | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | |
| 4. What I did made a difference to how the match went. `agency` | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | |
| 5. The match felt fair to my side. `fairness` | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | |
| 6. I enjoyed the match. `enjoyment` | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | |

**7. The time I had on my turn was:** too little / about right / too much `hadEnoughTime`

**8. Was there a moment when you did not know what was happening or what a rule meant? What was it?** `confusingMoments`

**11. Would you play again?** yes / no `wouldPlayAgain`

## Notes for the facilitator

- Hand the questionnaire out before the final reveal is discussed, and collect it before the debrief.
- Do not explain a rule while people are writing. Unanswered confusion is the data.
- Question 9 is entered in the facilitator's notes, since its numbers duplicate `agency` from the export; keep the reason the player gives.
- A player may skip any question. Enter `null`, not a guess.
- Free-text answers can identify a person. They stay in the restricted record and are quoted in a report only when they cannot be traced to a table.

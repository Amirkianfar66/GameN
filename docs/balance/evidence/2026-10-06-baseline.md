## 7. Seeded playouts

Two hundred seeded matches per mode, played by a random policy that chooses among the commands each player's view offers and also sends arbitrary commands. The invariants in [invariants.md](../invariants.md) were checked after every transition, and every playout was run twice.

```sh
npm run walk --workspace @mothership/balance -- --engine-root <copy> --engine-commit 8d4a2e5dc47eb827dbcbfd8382755db2fa3b0bde --seeds 200 --out docs/balance/evidence/2026-10-06-playouts-engine-8d4a2e5.json
```

| Mode | Playouts | Finished | Phases | Commands accepted | Commands refused | Invariant violations | Hint mismatches | Replay mismatches |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 7 players | 200 | 200 | 10108 | 16974 | 17490 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 8 players | 200 | 200 | 11230 | 18899 | 21970 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 9 players | 200 | 200 | 12314 | 20752 | 27599 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

- No invariant was violated in any mode.
- A hint mismatch would be a command the engine offered and then refused, or accepted without offering. There was none: the legal-target hints are exact for these playouts.
- Every playout reproduced its end state.

What the playouts reached, as coverage and nothing more:

| Reached at least once | 7 players | 8 players | 9 players |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A Blue result, a Red result and a Draw | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| An Alien co-win | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Alien winning alone | No | No | No |
| The showdown | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Most players Eliminated before a showdown, in any playout | 1 | 1 | 2 |
| Most players Jailed at once, in any playout | 5 | 5 | 5 |

These agree with the audit's counting: Alien never won alone (S-05), eliminations before the showdown stayed within 2, 2 and 3 (S-02), and five players can be in Jail (S-08). Agreement with 200 random playouts does not prove a bound; the bounds rest on the arithmetic.

The artifact also records the shortest and longest sum of 60-second windows among the playouts: 47 to 56, 48 to 62 and 55 to 66 minutes. They lie inside the clock arithmetic of the audit (S-13). They are sums of server windows under a random policy, not durations of matches played by people.

**How often a random policy reaches an outcome is not recorded, on purpose.** It would look like a win rate and would mean nothing: random players do not persuade, bluff or vote like people.

## 9. Corrections made while producing this evidence

Each is listed because a changed expectation is the easiest way to manufacture a pass. None of these changed an expectation to agree with the engine.

1. **An invariant was over-broad.** The first run failed six scenarios on INV-EL-03, which then said that an Eliminated player may never hold a special shot. A participant eliminated during the showdown keeps an unused shot flag in server state after the match. No rule forbids that: R-SHOW-02 grants shots when the showdown opens. The invariant now checks what the rule says, that nobody who was Eliminated before the showdown holds one.
2. **One scenario was scripted wrongly.** In the nine-player `SHOW-14` a player already Eliminated was listed as a voter. The engine refused the ballot, correctly. The voter list was fixed.
3. **The rulebook misstated Scan.** A draft of R-ROLE-13 read as if Hacker could Scan Undercover from another room. The baseline requires the same location for every other player. The rule was reworded and `SCAN-03` now covers it.
4. **The adapter did not report a pending Protection.** The engine holds a grant as a queued command until its round resolves. The binding now reports that as pending. This is a mapping, not an expectation.
5. **The audit's map of owner decisions missed one rule.** `npm run check` caught that R-FLOW-11 cites V1-10 and was not listed. Fixed.
6. **One control was not a real change.** Reversing an empty list of expected turns changes nothing. The control now appends a seat instead.

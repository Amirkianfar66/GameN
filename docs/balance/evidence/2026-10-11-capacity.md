# Evidence, 11 October 2026: the room capacity of five

What was actually run for the audit of issue #96, [capacity-audit.md](../capacity-audit.md), against Backend's implementation in PR #98. Every table below is printed from the reports beside this file, and a test holds this document to them.

**The result in one line.** Every case that an engine run decides passes against the implementation, except one case with each number of players: a healed player is sent to the wrong room when a Captain is hurt in the same round after entering Command Room (finding F1 of the audit).

**What these runs are.** Scripted cases with expectations written from the rule, and random playouts checked against invariants at every step. They show what the engine does and what can be reached.

**What they are not.** Nothing was played by people, and nothing here says how the rule changes the balance of the game. The service (the draw of starting rooms, two phones moving at once, lobbies and matches of earlier rulesets) was read and not run.

## Playouts under the capacity ruleset

200 seeded playouts for each number of players, each run twice, from starting rooms the rule allows, every legal assignment equally likely. The policy picks at random among what each player is offered, adds arbitrary commands, and in a quarter of a player's phases also tries to move into a room of five when there is one. After every command and every phase the invariants are checked, the six capacity invariants among them, and each player's offers are held against what the engine then accepts.

## How the fixtures came to be, in order

1. Written from issue #96 and the rulebook, and committed as `ce4d9d0` before Backend's commit was read: 85 fixtures, 65 of them ready.
2. Backend's commit and its record of two further decisions of the owner were read. Cases 24 and 26 (nine players) had been blocked and became ready on that record; case 27 was added, blocked, for a question the implementation raised. Committed as `8eb5cef`. Against the implementation: 67 passed, none failed.
3. An independent review of `8eb5cef` by a separate Claude Code agent, in a clone of its own. It found no wrong expectation and a situation the audit had called impossible, in which the implementation fails. Cases 28, 29 and 35 to 43 were added, the nine-player Hospital cases were rebuilt so that they tell the decided order from others, a check in case 13 that could not fail was dropped, and three invariants were added.

**The independent review.** A separate Claude Code agent, not a person, was given a clone of this branch at `8eb5cef`, the two built engines and the task of finding what was wrong, missing or overclaimed. It ran scripted cases of its own, some ten thousand playouts and copies of the engine with one change each.

| What it found | What was done |
| --- | --- |
| A Captain named in a room who then enters Command Room is hit inside it. The audit had called that impossible. The implementation then sends a healed player to the wrong room | Reproduced here with a separate script. Case 28, which fails; finding F1; BAL-REQ-13. Cases 29, 35, 42 and 43; question CAP-D5; sections 4.3, 4.5 and 4.7 of the audit corrected |
| The nine-player Hospital cases passed on a copy of the engine without the pass that frees healed places first, and on one that put patients out by number | Rebuilt: the patient with the highest number is the one healed (case 22) and the one who must keep a place (cases 21 and 24) |
| Nothing checked that a waiting player is ever admitted, or what Cracker can do for one | Cases 36, 37 and 38 |
| An Eliminated player's place against a forced return, a full room outside the ordinary turns, and a release and a healing for one place had no case | Cases 39, 40 and 41 |
| The service draws a room when a player asks to join, not when they are approved; a player can ask again for a new draw; players approved last start together more often only with eight and nine | Section 4.1 and finding F2 say so. The first point had already been corrected from a closer reading before the review came in |
| No invariant saw a player moved who should have stayed, and halves of the capacity invariants had no static test | INV-CAP-04 to 06; INV-CAP-03 covers a Captain who lost the title; a static test for each half |

It found no expectation that a correct engine could fail, and no way to put a sixth player anywhere or to stop a match.

Afterwards 43 single changes to the tooling, the fixtures and the documents were tried against the static tests in a scratch clone. 41 were noticed at once; for the other two a test was added, and they are noticed now.

The changes made after the review have not themselves been reviewed independently.

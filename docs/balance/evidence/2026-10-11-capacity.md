# Evidence, 11 October 2026: the room capacity of five

What was actually run for the audit of issue #96, [capacity-audit.md](../capacity-audit.md), against Backend's implementation in PR #98. Every table below is printed from the reports beside this file, and a test holds this document to them.

**The result in short.** Of the 107 cases an engine run decides, 92 pass against the implementation and 15 fail. Three of the failures are one case with each number of players and are a defect: a healed player is sent to the wrong room when a Captain is hurt in the same round after entering Command Room (finding F1 of the audit). The other 12 are four cases with each number of players that hold rules the owner decided on 11 October, after the implementation was written (findings F3, F4, F5 and F11). The earlier rulesets play on the new build as before.

**What these runs are.** Scripted cases with expectations written from the rule, and random playouts checked against invariants at every step. They show what the engine does and what can be reached.

**What they are not.** Nothing was played by people, and nothing here says how the rule changes the balance of the game. The service (the draw of starting rooms, two phones moving at once, lobbies and matches of earlier rulesets) was read and not run.

All five cases are about where a returning player is seated, and in every failing run a capacity invariant names the same thing: INV-CAP-03, or INV-CAP-07 for the tie between two returning players.

- **Case 28, the defect.** Room B is the healed player's last room. It holds four players before the round resolves and four after it; in between, the implementation sets the Captain who was hurt inside Command Room down in it. The engine before the rule passes this case's own checks.
- **Case 25** (`BAL-OD-2`). A room-mate leaves the healed player's last room for Hospital in the same round. The owner decided that the place counts as free; the implementation seats the healed player before the room-mate has left.
- **Case 27** (`BAL-OD-3`). A player was sent to the other room, was hurt there and is healed. The owner decided that the last room is the room the player was last in; the implementation returns them to the room they could not enter earlier.
- **Case 45** (`BAL-OD-4`). A Captain loses the title inside Command Room while a room-mate leaves the Captain's last room for Hospital. The owner decided that such a Captain is seated once the round has resolved; the implementation seats them at the moment they are hit.
- **Case 46** (`BAL-OD-6`). A healed player and a Captain Eliminated inside Command Room have the same last room, with one place. The owner decided that the lower player number gets it, here the healed player; the implementation seats the Captain first. In case 44 the Captain has the lower number, and it passes.

**The control.** Against the engine before the rule 104 of the 107 ready capacity cases fail. Three pass: `V1-M7-CAP-29`, `V1-M7-CAP-42`, `V1-M8-CAP-29`. In each of them a Captain who lost the title comes to a last room that has a place, which is so with or without the rule, and with that number of players no room of five occurs in the case. Of those that fail, 66 fail on what they assert themselves. The other 38 assert nothing an engine without the rule gets wrong (a start, a room with a place, the Jail, the Final Zone, a return to a room that is not full) and fail only because that engine offers a full room to somebody from the first moment, which the capacity invariants catch.

**The negative controls.** For every ready case that passes, each of its expectations is made wrong in turn, and the run must then fail. This tests the fixtures, not the engine.

## Playouts under the capacity ruleset

200 seeded playouts for each number of players, each run twice, from starting rooms the rule allows, every legal assignment equally likely. The policy picks at random among what each player is offered, adds arbitrary commands, and in a quarter of a player's phases also tries to move into a room of five when there is one. After every command and every phase the invariants are checked, the seven capacity invariants among them, and each player's offers are held against what the engine then accepts.

Two of those invariants, INV-CAP-03 and INV-CAP-07, hold the owner's decisions of 11 October on returns, which the implementation predates. Where random play reaches a return of that kind, the invariant is broken; the table says which and how often. ## How the fixtures came to be, in order

1. Written from issue #96 and the rulebook, and committed as `ce4d9d0` before Backend's commit was read: 85 fixtures, 65 of them ready.
2. Backend's commit and its record of two further decisions of the owner were read. Cases 24 and 26 (nine players) had been blocked and became ready on that record; case 27 was added, blocked, for a question the implementation raised. Committed as `8eb5cef`. Against the implementation: 67 passed, none failed.
3. An independent review of `8eb5cef` by a separate Claude Code agent, in a clone of its own. It found no wrong expectation and a situation the audit had called impossible, in which the implementation fails. Cases 28, 29 and 35 to 43 were added, the nine-player Hospital cases were rebuilt so that they tell the decided order from others, a check in case 13 that could not fail was dropped, and three invariants were added. Committed as `ca18852`, with evidence in `1018831`: 85 passed, 3 failed.
4. The owner decided the three open questions and when starting rooms are drawn, on 11 October, in the Game Balance session (audit, section 7). Cases 25, 27, 42 and 43 had been blocked and became ready; case 45 was added; case 44 was added, blocked, for the one question those decisions raised. The invariant on returns was made to hold the decisions, and keeps each player's last room itself. Committed as `bcbfcd9`, with evidence in `115fe13`: 89 passed, 12 failed.
5. The owner decided that last question too, the same day: the lower player number. Case 44 became ready, case 46 was added, and an invariant was added for the tie. This report is of the runs made after that.

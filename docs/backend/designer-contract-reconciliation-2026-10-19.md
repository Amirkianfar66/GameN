## S3: answers to the Designer's three confirmations

Answers the exact [question on #153](https://github.com/Amirkianfar66/GameN/pull/153#issuecomment-6254883524) and [S3 handoff section 8](https://github.com/Amirkianfar66/GameN/blob/cb9386bd716dc346160509d5e49d7348fdaf4f84/docs/design/clone-s3.md#L129-L135).

1. **Normal expiry — confirmed for an activated Clone.** Its last covered normal round is `min(activatedRound + 1, 5)`. `finishCloneCheckpoint()` clears `active` after the complete normal attack/medical/placement batch, before victory and the next phase. The first resulting own view has `active:false`, retaining `used:true`, activation and original expiry metadata. There is no valid intermediate projection with a half-completed checkpoint. See [Clone expiry](https://github.com/Amirkianfar66/GameN/blob/6ca4b51d5acd1454daec68c5108c8f12ee2286bf/packages/engine/src/full-game/clone.ts#L92-L97) and [full-stage lifecycle](https://github.com/Amirkianfar66/GameN/blob/6ca4b51d5acd1454daec68c5108c8f12ee2286bf/packages/engine/src/full-game/lifecycle.ts#L172-L199). An expiry film still requires a live own-view transition; a local deadline or recovered old activation receipt is insufficient.
2. **Elimination — confirmed.** The transition that publishes the Alien as Eliminated also publishes its Clone inactive. Immediate EJECT and end-round damage/death cleanup happen before projection. The consumer may remove the private card using the own seat's Eliminated health and suppress expiry art; Backend retains the seat, spent-use metadata and history. See [elimination cleanup](https://github.com/Amirkianfar66/GameN/blob/6ca4b51d5acd1454daec68c5108c8f12ee2286bf/packages/engine/src/full-game/clone.ts#L84-L89) and [post-command cleanup](https://github.com/Amirkianfar66/GameN/blob/6ca4b51d5acd1454daec68c5108c8f12ee2286bf/packages/engine/src/full-game/lifecycle.ts#L340-L342).
3. **Match end — confirmed with a wording correction.** **If still active when the match finishes or aborts, Clone clears in that same authoritative FINISHED/ABORTED view. Terminal cleanup does not publish a separate earlier deactivation view. Normal expiry or an earlier elimination may already have cleared it.** “In no earlier view” is therefore valid only for terminal-caused cleanup, not a promise that Clone always remains active until match end. An unused Clone stays `used:false`. See [terminal phase construction](https://github.com/Amirkianfar66/GameN/blob/6ca4b51d5acd1454daec68c5108c8f12ee2286bf/packages/engine/src/full-game/lifecycle.ts#L37-L40) and [stopCloneEffects](https://github.com/Amirkianfar66/GameN/blob/6ca4b51d5acd1454daec68c5108c8f12ee2286bf/packages/engine/src/full-game/clone.ts#L100-L105). At normal R5 completion expiry precedes either SHOWDOWN or FINISHED. SHOWDOWN is live gameplay, not `matchEnded`; it has no active Clone.

Only the Alien's own `self.cloneStatus` contains the object; non-Alien own statuses are null and the public projection omits it. The [persisted private-view refinement](https://github.com/Amirkianfar66/GameN/blob/6ca4b51d5acd1454daec68c5108c8f12ee2286bf/packages/contracts/src/full-game.ts#L159-L166) and [own/public projections](https://github.com/Amirkianfar66/GameN/blob/6ca4b51d5acd1454daec68c5108c8f12ee2286bf/packages/engine/src/full-game/lifecycle.ts#L423-L437) enforce this boundary. The [proposed S3 trigger](https://github.com/Amirkianfar66/GameN/blob/cb9386bd716dc346160509d5e49d7348fdaf4f84/design/board-v2/films/clone.mjs#L159-L182) can use `live`, own Eliminated health and FINISHED/ABORTED to suppress inappropriate films without a new shared field. ## #158 clinical anchors and snapshot occupancy

The [service allocator](https://github.com/Amirkianfar66/GameN/blob/6ca4b51d5acd1454daec68c5108c8f12ee2286bf/services/game-api/src/full-game.ts#L27-L46) reserves each starting Hospital occupant's HV geometry, including bodies, then assigns distinct anchors to new clinical subjects in numeric seat order. The anchor is presentation metadata, not the subject's final physical slot. Healing may free H1 for another patient while the healed subject still carries HV1. Original wire schemas still require a valid anchor for every medical fact.

The [Hospital film handoff](https://github.com/Amirkianfar66/GameN/blob/7cf140aff60374a99b3ac83e2c67b7cf6916348e/docs/design/hospital-films.md#L79-L90) and [filmBerth implementation](https://github.com/Amirkianfar66/GameN/blob/7cf140aff60374a99b3ac83e2c67b7cf6916348e/design/board-v2/films/medical.mjs#L80-L92) agree on these consumer boundaries:

- Persistent Injured/critical state and its entry use the admitted patient's actual Hospital `slotId`; do not relocate it to match a vignette anchor.
- HEALED/DEATH imagery may use the anchor's berth only if another stable seat does not hold it in the published snapshot. A retained body counts as a holder. Otherwise preserve the authorized words without painting over that occupant or its target.
- The helper expects normalized `{seat, slotId}` records and its `subject` in the same identity space. Wire snapshots use `seatId`. **Map `seatId` explicitly to that normalized identity, including bodies, or port the check using `seatId`; do not pass raw wire seats to this helper.** Its `?.seat ?? null` would otherwise hide an existing holder and incorrectly permit the film. No new protocol field is needed.
- Bind words/film to a fresh permitted occurrence and its committed view. Current snapshot placement remains authority after missed/expired/baseline-suppressed events. Newer occupancy, privacy concealment and Blackout ownership cancel imagery. Multiple clinical facts for one subject, simultaneous subjects and cancellation remain consumer acceptance cases, not a new server timeline.

## #159 RELEASE / EJECT seat-list semantics

The exact [outcome producer](https://github.com/Amirkianfar66/GameN/blob/6ca4b51d5acd1454daec68c5108c8f12ee2286bf/packages/engine/src/full-game/lifecycle.ts#L281-L285) records affected seats **before** the command. RELEASE lists living physical-Jail occupants. EJECT lists **every physical-Jail occupant, including already Eliminated bodies**. The [Captain command](https://github.com/Amirkianfar66/GameN/blob/6ca4b51d5acd1454daec68c5108c8f12ee2286bf/packages/engine/src/full-game/captain.ts#L79-L109) requires at least one living detained physical-Jail prisoner to enable either command; RELEASE frees those prisoners, while EJECT clears all physical-Jail occupants to Eliminated/null/null with current detention and medical episode cleared. A Hospital detainee is excluded from both physical-Jail lists. An empty/body-only Jail does not enable a new EJECT.

These are different sets: EJECT's `CAPTAIN_OUTCOME.seatIds` is the full cleared set; its pending faction-reveal list contains the previously living detained prisoners only. Do not substitute one set for the other or infer a new death for an already dead body. EJECT does not generate an ordinary clinical DEATH for each swept seat. Registered actions, original identities/Code and receipts survive removal; victory keeps its existing later complete-stage checkpoint.

[Captain handoff](https://github.com/Amirkianfar66/GameN/blob/7f3344ede87e8291c34601e649ca4ec91432b4db/docs/design/captain-films.md#L79-L88) and [film inputs](https://github.com/Amirkianfar66/GameN/blob/7f3344ede87e8291c34601e649ca4ec91432b4db/design/board-v2/films/captain.mjs#L6-L22) already match the full EJECT set: living subjects are silhouettes and bodies grey cut-outs, each located using held-before and committed-after snapshots. Do not narrow that event to living-only or include Hospital patients. If the prior snapshot was not held, do not reconstruct a Jail origin; render current placement and authorized words. The off-board plaque and Code choices derive from complete retained seats with null placement, not a count of EJECT events.

## Required counterexamples

- Original registers a spaceship hack, then Beta forks from that state. Selecting Beta retains the hack and its spent flag. Selecting a reality predating it retains neither. Do not refund merely because Original was not selected.
- A meeting Promise viewed as fulfilled in a speculative A location is Not fulfilled if the synced position is B when voting begins.
- An all-Eliminated Original does not end Blackhole before choosing between it and another reality with survivors.
- A valid Jail ballot can fulfil a Promise even if a full Jail prevents admission.
- RELEASE/EJECT do not erase a detained Hospital patient's reservation merely because the button was used.
- An accepted picture is not broadcast outside its window and a globally retained Promise is not published to other seats.
